FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it cost anything to join?

No. TikTok pays agencies for finding and developing LIVE talent. We will never invoice you, charge a fee, or touch your earnings.

Do you take a percentage of my gifts or diamonds?

No — your gift earnings stay yours. TikTok rewards Powerhouse separately based on how much our creators grow, which keeps our incentives exactly aligned with yours.

I've never gone LIVE before. Can I still apply?

Yes. We develop talent at every level — and if you're under 1,000 followers, we can often unlock LIVE access for promising applicants through the agency program.

What happens after I apply?

We review every application and reply within about 48 hours. If it looks like a fit, we'll set up a quick call, then send your official agency invitation inside TikTok.

Am I locked into anything?

You join through TikTok's official LIVE agency system, and we walk you through every term in plain English before you accept. Nothing is signed blind — no surprises.
